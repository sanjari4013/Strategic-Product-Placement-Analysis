# 📊 Strategic Product Placement Analysis

A Tableau-based Business Intelligence project that analyzes retail sales data to understand how product placement strategies influence customer purchasing behavior and overall sales performance.

---

# 📖 Project Overview

Strategic Product Placement Analysis is a data analytics project developed using **Tableau Desktop**. The project transforms retail sales data into interactive dashboards and stories that provide meaningful insights into product placement, customer behavior, promotional effectiveness, competitor pricing, seasonal demand, and foot traffic.

The dashboard enables retailers and business analysts to identify sales trends, optimize product placement strategies, and make informed business decisions using visual analytics.

---

# 🎯 Purpose

The primary purpose of this project is to analyze retail sales data and identify effective product placement strategies that improve product visibility, customer engagement, and overall business performance.

---

# ❗ Problem Statement

Retail businesses often struggle to determine the most effective placement strategy for products within stores. Poor product positioning can reduce product visibility, decrease customer engagement, and negatively impact sales performance.

This project analyzes retail sales data using Tableau and provides business insights through interactive dashboards to support data-driven retail decision-making.

---

# 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze retail sales performance using Tableau Desktop.
- Study the impact of product placement on sales.
- Evaluate promotional campaign effectiveness.
- Compare competitor pricing strategies.
- Analyze customer demographics and purchasing behavior.
- Study seasonal sales trends.
- Analyze foot traffic and sales relationship.
- Develop interactive dashboards and Tableau Stories.
- Publish dashboards using Tableau Public.
- Maintain project documentation using GitHub.
