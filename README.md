# Strategic Product Placement Analysis

## Project Overview
Strategic Product Placement Analysis is a Tableau-based data visualization project designed to analyze the impact of product placement strategies on retail sales performance. The project focuses on understanding how factors such as product category, promotions, competitor pricing, seasonal trends, and customer foot traffic influence customer purchasing behavior and sales volume.

The project utilizes Tableau Desktop for dashboard development and Tableau Public for publishing interactive visualizations and stories that help businesses make data-driven decisions.



## Purpose
The primary purpose of this project is to identify effective product placement strategies that improve product visibility, customer engagement, and overall sales performance in retail environments.


## Problem Statement
Retail businesses often struggle to determine the most effective placement strategy for products within stores. Improper product placement can reduce product visibility, customer engagement, and sales performance. This project aims to analyze product placement factors and provide recommendations to improve retail sales using data visualization techniques.



## Objectives
- Analyze the impact of product placement on sales volume.
- Study the effect of promotions on customer purchasing behavior.
- Evaluate competitor pricing strategies.
- Analyze seasonal trends affecting product demand.
- Understand the relationship between foot traffic and sales performance.
- Generate business recommendations for optimal product placement.
